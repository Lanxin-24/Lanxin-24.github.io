About Me

I am a master's student in Biostatistics at the University of Washington. Before joining UW, I received my B.S. in Information and Computing Science from the School of Mathematical Sciences at Fudan University.

Research Interests

Research

Single-cell Allele-specific Expression (ASE)

Investigating statistical and computational methods for analyzing allele-specific expression in single-cell RNA sequencing.

Gene Selection and Cell Classification in Single-cell RNA-seq

Developed a two-stage framework combining rank-based convex optimization and differentiable sampling for marker gene selection, cell-type classification, and phenotype reconstruction. Explored dimensionality reduction and unsupervised clustering to identify cellular subpopulations and visualize spatial gene expression patterns.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Biomedical Data

Evaluated retrieval-augmented generation models for biomedical question answering and disease-type identification, and developed evaluation pipelines to support biomedical literature synthesis.
